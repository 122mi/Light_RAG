# --- Part I Core & Foundational Concepts ---

八字  has_pinyin  bā zì
八字  has_english_name  The Eight Characters  Bazi
八字  is_in_category  核心与基础概念
八字  has_explanation  A Chinese astrological system that analyzes a person's destiny and personality based on the eight characters representing their birth year, month, day, and hour.
八字  is_also_known_as  四柱命理学
四柱命理学  has_pinyin  sì zhù mìng lǐ xué
四柱命理学  has_english_name  The Study of Four Pillars of Destiny
四柱命理学  is_in_category  核心与基础概念
四柱命理学  has_explanation  The formal name for Bazi, emphasizing the analysis of the four pillars that constitute a person's fate.
日主  has_pinyin  rì zhǔ
日主  has_english_name  Day Master  Day Stem
日主  is_in_category  核心与基础概念
日主  has_explanation  The Heavenly Stem of the Day Pillar. It is the most important character in the chart as it represents the self, the core personality of the individual.
命盘  has_pinyin  mìng pán
命盘  has_english_name  Bazi Chart  Natal Chart
命盘  is_in_category  核心与基础概念
命盘  has_explanation  The complete chart of the Four Pillars, laying out the eight characters and supplementary information such as Luck Pillars and Ten Gods.
大运  has_pinyin  dà yùn
大运  has_english_name  Luck Pillar  Decade Luck
大运  is_in_category  核心与基础概念
大运  has_explanation  Represents a ten-year period of a person's life, governed by a specific pair of Heavenly Stem and Earthly Branch, indicating the major influences during that decade.
流年  has_pinyin  liú nián
流年  has_english_name  Annual Pillar  Passing Year
流年  is_in_category  核心与基础概念
流年  has_explanation  The Heavenly Stem and Earthly Branch of a particular year, which interacts with the natal chart and Luck Pillars to influence a person's fortune for that year.

# --- Part II The Four Pillars (四柱) ---

四柱  has_pinyin  sì zhù
四柱  has_english_name  The Four Pillars
四柱  is_in_category  四柱
四柱  has_explanation  The foundational structure of a Bazi chart, consisting of the Year Pillar, Month Pillar, Day Pillar, and Hour Pillar.
四柱  includes_concept  年柱
四柱  includes_concept  月柱
四柱  includes_concept  日柱
四柱  includes_concept  时柱
年柱  has_pinyin  nián zhù
年柱  has_english_name  Year Pillar
年柱  is_type_of  四柱
年柱  represents  Ancestors, parents, early life (childhood), external environment, social circle.
月柱  has_pinyin  yuè zhù
月柱  has_english_name  Month Pillar
月柱  is_type_of  四柱
月柱  represents  Parents, siblings, career, work environment, formative years (youth). It also indicates the season of birth.
日柱  has_pinyin  rì zhù
日柱  has_english_name  Day Pillar
日柱  is_type_of  四柱
日柱  represents  The self (Day Master) and the spouse (Day Branch), middle age, one's own body, and intimate relationships.
时柱  has_pinyin  shí zhù
时柱  has_english_name  Hour Pillar
时柱  is_type_of  四柱
时柱  represents  Children, subordinates, investments, ambitions, and later life (old age).
